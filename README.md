# Global Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyses Global Superstore sales data using Power BI to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product trends and regional contribution. The interactive dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful insights to support business decision-making.

## Objectives
- Analyse overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Compare sales across regions, markets and customer segments.
- Understand sales trends and profitability.
- Identify growth opportunities and areas requiring improvement.

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQL:** Data querying and analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development.
- **DAX:** KPI calculations and measures.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Source data.

## Dataset
The project uses the Global Superstore dataset, containing order-level sales information.

Key fields include:
- Order ID and Order Date
- Product Category and Sub-Category
- Sales, Profit and Quantity
- Region, Market and Country
- Customer Segment
- Shipping Mode and Shipping Cost

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Sales
- Profit Margin
- Shipping Costs

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for overall sales and profitability.
- Sales trend analysis over time.
- Regional and market-wise sales comparisons.
- Product category and sub-category performance.
- Customer segment analysis.
- Interactive slicers for exploring sales data.

## Business Analysis
The dashboard helps identify high-performing markets, profitable product categories, sales trends and areas where performance may require attention. It also enables comparisons between sales revenue and profit to avoid relying on sales volume alone.

## Business Recommendations
- Strengthen strategies in high-performing regions.
- Investigate low-performing markets and product categories.
- Monitor profit margins alongside revenue.
- Improve product and inventory planning based on sales trends.
- Use interactive filters to support targeted business decisions.

## Conclusion
The Global Superstore Sales Dashboard demonstrates how Power BI can convert raw sales data into actionable business insights. It supports performance monitoring, profitability analysis and informed decisions regarding products, markets and customers.

**Note:** Findings and recommendations are based on the data and filters selected in the dashboard.
